Who the hell invented LARP?
No one does. Still, enjoy the chaos.
1 · THE PARADOX
Put a LARP in a gallery and it dies. No one wants to be a wizard next to a bloke in a blazer drinking wine. The Art-LARP Paradox: fancy word for “gallery ruined it”.
1.5 · SOURCE?
Allegedly named by Alex Brown (2025, Knutepunkt). Source: trust me, bro, but check it.
2 · THE HOBBY
Late 70s and 80s: America, Europe and Australia all went “let’s hit each other with foam swords” without telling each other.
2.5 · 1977
Dagorhir (1977) is the classic early one.
2.9 · THE NAME
“LARP” came into being around 1990, really as a result of the sheer difficulty of coming up with a name for it.
3 · THE SLANG
To be called a LARPer now is to be called a complete and utter bastard and a costume-wearer. No one invented it.
3.5 · HOW IT SPREAD
It was the forums that turned it into an insult, and then the short video apps took it up and ran with it like a rash.
THE END
So who gets the credit? The hobby: naff. The slang: naff. The paradox: Alex Brown, apparently. Well done Alex. 🏆
